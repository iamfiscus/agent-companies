Start of sprint planning:

1. CEO identifies highest priority work items
2. Lead Researcher assigns research agents to gather context
3. CTO reviews architectural implications
4. `ce:brainstorm` for any new features
5. `ce:plan` to produce implementation plans
6. CTO approves plans or sends back for revision
7. VP Engineering receives approved plans for the Work phase
