The leadership team does not write code. They own the compound loop — ensuring Plan feeds from Research, Work is unblocked, Review catches real issues, and Compound captures learnings.

- **CEO** — Coordinates the full cycle. Approves plans and hires.
- **CTO** — Owns architecture decisions and agent-native compliance.
- **VP Engineering** — Owns execution during the Work phase.
- **VP Quality** — Owns multi-agent review during the Review phase.
- **VP Knowledge** — Owns learning capture during the Compound phase.
